Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how MediCare collects, uses, protects, and handles your information.

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the MediCare digital healthcare platform and describes how information provided through the platform may be handled when you create an account, manage appointments, or use healthcare services.

01

Information We Collect

When you create and use a MediCare account, we may collect information necessary to provide healthcare-related services and maintain your account.

  • Full name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Account credentials
  • Appointment and scheduling information
  • Information you voluntarily provide through the platform
02

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through MediCare may be used to operate and improve the platform and provide requested healthcare services.

  • Creating and managing your patient account
  • Managing appointments and scheduling
  • Communicating important account information
  • Improving platform functionality and user experience
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing misuse
03

Healthcare Information

Healthcare and medical information is sensitive. Access to healthcare information should be limited to authorized users and healthcare professionals who require it for legitimate purposes.

MediCare is designed to provide controlled access to healthcare-related information through authenticated accounts and appropriate access controls.

04

Account Security

We take reasonable measures to protect account information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and should notify the appropriate system administrator if you believe your account has been compromised.

05

Information Sharing

Personal information should only be accessed or shared when necessary to provide services, operate the platform, meet legitimate administrative requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not intend to sell your personal information.

06

Cookies and Local Storage

The platform may use browser storage and similar technologies to maintain session information, preferences, and essential application functionality.

These technologies should only be used for purposes necessary for operating and improving the platform.

07

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws and the nature of the information involved, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

  • Request access to information associated with your account
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally applicable
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled
08

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the MediCare platform, operational practices, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will display a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Questions about privacy?

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the MediCare administration team.
